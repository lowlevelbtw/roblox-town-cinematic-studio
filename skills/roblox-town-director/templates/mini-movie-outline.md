# Mini-Movie Production Outline Template (3–10+ Minutes)

Use this template for longer Roblox stories and episodic dramas without forcing rushed Shorts pacing.

## 1. Concept, Production Method & Dual-Gate Verdict
- **Working Title / Genre / Target Runtime:** (Default to `16:9` widescreen unless a vertical series is requested)
- **Production Method Required:** [Live Gameplay Staging / Custom Roblox Animation / Hybrid-External] *(list exact required capabilities + lower-resource alternative if needed)*
- **Characters & Visual Contrast:** [Core goals, flaws, relationships, and distinct wardrobe silhouettes]
- **Experience & Setup Plan:** [Chosen game or Studio project, server/scene continuity plan, and cast/production setup]
- **Gate A (Story Quality)** & **Gate B (Production Feasibility)** ratings with 1-sentence reasons.

## 2. Three-Act Narrative Architecture
- **Act 1 (Inciting Disruption):** Protagonist desire, world dynamics, and disruption within 15–30 seconds.
- **Act 2 (Rising Stakes & Midpoint Shift):** 2–3 escalating complications driven by character choices, midpoint reversal, and low point.
- **Act 3 (Climax & Resolution):** Final choice and grounded visual/emotional payoff.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Breakdown & Visual Direction
For each scene:
- **Scene # | Location & Lighting Lock**
- **Dramatic Purpose & Visual Motif** (staging, camera perspective, spatial tension)
- **Characters & Continuity Notes** (wardrobe, prop state, 180-degree screen direction)
- **Key Actions** (verified or marked `UNVERIFIED` with a 1-Min In-Game Check & fallback)
- **Raw Duration → Edited Duration** *(Record enough additional footage to allow clean editing. Recommend 2–3 seconds of handles where practical, but adapt to the shot and recording method.)*

## 4. Production Execution Schedule
- **Location-Grouped Shooting / Rendering:** Group all shots in the same map location or scene setup together (out of story order) to protect lighting, set, and prop continuity.
- **Environment-Adapted Session Plan:** Run a brief technical test clip (framing, stability, audio, storage) before full recording or rendering. Structure session length and breaks around the creator's hardware and cast schedule (for example, shorter modular blocks with cool-down breaks if recording on thermally constrained mobile devices, or scene-based batches on desktop/Studio).

## 5. Post-Production, Sound Design & Packaging
- **Editing & Pacing:** Scene-level rhythm, cutting on action, dialogue/VO radio edit, and 4-layer sound plan (Dialogue/VO, Ambience, Foley/SFX, Music & Strategic Silence).
- **Export & Packaging:** Brief test export before full render, 3 honest title options, `16:9` thumbnail composition, and Stage L QC check.
