# Illustrative Example: Solo 45s Suspense-Twist Short

*Note: This illustrative example demonstrates how `roblox-town-director` (`FULL` mode) develops a story from creative direction through execution for a creator who specifically asks for a solo Android Live Gameplay Staging shoot. It has **not** been physically filmed or field-tested yet.*

**User Prompt:** "I want to make a 45-second suspense Short about a character who breaks a house rule by opening a locked upstairs door. I'm filming solo on an Android phone (8GB RAM, no PC) and haven't picked a Roblox town game yet."

---

## 1. Story, Production Method & Dual-Gate Verdict
- **Production Method Required:** **Live Gameplay Staging** (achievable in an existing Roblox town experience without Roblox Studio, custom rigs, or external 3D animation, using a controlled edit cut for the final door-closure illusion).
- **Experience & Setup (Labeled Assumption):** Experience-agnostic (targets a Roblox town house with an interior door; door open/close/auto-close mechanics, hallway-vs-room lighting contrast, and portrait capture framing are all **`UNVERIFIED`** until tested in-game via a **1-Min In-Game Check**). Adapted for **solo 1-device Android capture** per the user's prompt.
- **Visual Direction Note:** Lead character is a curious new tenant (casual everyday wardrobe) framed against narrow hallway geometry—using lit-hallway spill light and doorway silhouettes rather than monster jumpscares.
- **Gate A — Story & Creative Quality:**
  - Hook: `STRONG` — Opens *in medias res* on a concrete rule violation and clear visual focal point (the closed upstairs door).
  - Clarity: `STRONG` — One rule ("never open the upstairs closet"), immediate curiosity, and clear spatial stakes.
  - Character & Escalation: `NEEDS WORK` — Breaking the rule on pure curiosity is thin; the impossible knock from inside helps, and adding a personal trigger (e.g., hearing their own phone chime from behind the door) would strengthen motivation.
  - Originality: `NEEDS WORK` — The "forbidden room / locked-inside" horror setup is a familiar trope; it relies on tight spatial staging, lighting contrast, and sound design rather than a fresh premise.
  - Payoff: `NEEDS WORK` — The trap twist only lands if the door clearly shuts without the protagonist touching it; in solo live gameplay, this depends on either an auto-closing door (`UNVERIFIED`) or a seamless jump-cut illusion in post.
- **Gate B — Production Feasibility:**
  - Production Method Match: `STRONG` — Fits Live Gameplay Staging with zero custom VFX.
  - Cast & Coordination: `STRONG` — 100% solo; uses 4 short takes across two doorway angles.
  - Blocking & Actions: `STRONG` — Uses only walking, pausing, turning, and an off-camera door reset between Takes 3A and 3B.
  - Experience Capabilities: `NEEDS WORK` — Interior door open/close and auto-close behavior are `UNVERIFIED` until checked in the chosen house.
  - Post & Audio Scope: `STRONG` — Voiceover + 3 punctuation SFX (knock, creak, slam/lock) + `0.5s` silence + one locked-camera cut.

## 2. Final Script, Visual Intent, Captions & Sound Timing (45.0s Exact)
*Burned-in captions in the upper-center safe zone ensure the story works even with sound muted:*

- **`0:00.0–0:03.0` (Take 1 — Hook):** *Visual:* Tight over-the-shoulder framing on the closed door. | **VO:** "There was one warning taped to the upstairs closet when I moved in: never open it." | **Caption:** `"RULE #1: NEVER OPEN THIS DOOR."` | **Sound:** Quiet room tone.
- **`0:03.0–0:15.5` (Take 1 cont. — Escalation):** *Visual:* Avatar takes one slow step forward and freezes on the hallway mark. | **VO:** "The house was empty. I was completely alone. Then something knocked—from the inside." | **Caption:** `"I WAS ALONE. THEN SOMETHING KNOCKED."` | **Sound:** Knocking SFX at `0:12.0`.
- **`0:15.5–0:18.0` (Take 1 end — Door Opens):** *Visual:* Avatar taps the door open at `0:15.5` (`UNVERIFIED` tap/open mechanic). | **VO:** "I finally pulled the handle open..." | **Caption:** `"I PULLED IT OPEN..."` | **Sound:** Door creak SFX at `0:15.5`.
- **`0:18.0–0:26.0` (Take 2 — Turn):** *Visual:* Side angle as avatar crosses the threshold into the inner room, leaving the door open behind them. | **VO:** "...and stepped inside." | **Caption:** `"AND STEPPED INSIDE."` | **Sound:** Slow footsteps SFX starting at `0:18.5`.
- **`0:26.0–0:36.5` (Take 3A — Empty Space):** *Visual:* Inside the room looking back past the protagonist toward the wide-open doorway and lit hallway. Protagonist stands visibly away from the door, hands empty. | **VO:** "Nothing. Just an empty room." | **Caption:** `"NOTHING. JUST AN EMPTY ROOM."` | **Sound:** Room tone drops into **0.5s total silence at the end of Take 3A (`0:36.0–0:36.5`)**.
- **`0:36.5–0:45.0` (Take 3B — Payoff):** *Visual:* Hard cut at `0:36.5` on the exact same locked camera angle: the door is now slammed shut (or swings shut independently if auto-close is available), cutting off the hallway light while the protagonist remains frozen inside. | **VO:** "I let out a breath—until the door slammed shut behind me. Wait... if I'm inside, who just locked it?" | **Caption:** `"WAIT... WHO JUST LOCKED IT?"` | **Sound:** Sharp door slam + lock-click SFX begins **exactly on the `0:36.5` cut** (not before it).

## 3. Characters, Continuity Lock & Pre-Shoot Check
- **Continuity Lock:** Lock avatar outfit and indoor lighting before Take 1; aim for hallway-vs-inner-room silhouette contrast (`UNVERIFIED` in-game lighting behaviour—fallback: rely on clear open-vs-closed doorframe geometry if interior lighting is flat).
- **1-Min In-Game Check:** Confirm whether the upstairs door stays open, closes on tap, or auto-closes (`UNVERIFIED`).
  - *If auto-close exists:* Record Take 3 as a single continuous shot while the protagonist stands visibly away from the door and lets it close on its own.
  - *If tap-only (default solo fallback):* Never show the protagonist touching the door in the twist beat. Use the locked-camera Take 3A (door open) → Take 3B (door closed) edit cut with the slam SFX starting on the cut at `0:36.5`.

## 4. Scene & Shot Plan (In Recording Order)
*(Record enough additional footage to allow clean editing. Recommend 2–3 seconds of handles where practical, adapting to each shot. - **Take 1 (Raw ~21s → Edit 18.0s, `0:00.0–0:18.0`) — Hallway Approach & Door Open:** Over-the-shoulder behind avatar facing the closed door (*Fallback:* stationary camera in hallway, walk avatar into frame). Hold opening hook (`0:00.0–0:03.0`), step onto hallway floor mark and hold through the `0:12.0` knock cue, then tap door open at **`0:15.5`** (`UNVERIFIED` door tap behaviour) to match the `0:15.5` door creak SFX.
- **Take 2 (Raw ~11s → Edit 8.0s, `0:18.0–0:26.0`) — Crossing the Threshold:** Frame beside the doorframe looking across the threshold. - **Take 3A (Raw ~13s → Edit 10.5s, `0:26.0–0:36.5`) — Inside Room (Door Open):** Stand inside the room on a fixed floor mark, clearly away from the door, facing the open doorway and lit hallway. Hold still for 10.5s with no hands near the door.
- **Take 3B (Raw ~11s → Edit 8.5s, `0:36.5–0:45.0`) — Inside Room (Door Shut Illusion):** Close the door off-camera, return to the exact same floor mark and camera angle, and hold still in the suddenly dim room. *(Skip 3B only if your 1-Min In-Game Check confirms an auto-closing door that shuts cleanly while you stand on the mark.)*
